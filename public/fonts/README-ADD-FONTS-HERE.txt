Self-hosted fonts (so visitors' IP addresses are NOT sent to Google)

Put these 5 files in this folder (names must match exactly):
  forum-regular.woff2      (Forum, Regular 400)
  limelight-regular.woff2  (Limelight, Regular 400)
  jost-300.woff2           (Jost 300)
  jost-400.woff2           (Jost 400)
  jost-500.woff2           (Jost 500)

How: on YOUR computer open https://gwfh.mranftl.com/fonts (google-webfonts-helper),
choose each font, tick subsets "cyrillic" + "latin" (cyrillic is needed for Mongolian),
download, rename to the names above. Downloading once yourself is fine; the point is
that your VISITORS never contact Google.

All three fonts are under the SIL Open Font License, which allows self-hosting.
Keep their license text (OFL.txt) in this folder.

Until the files are added, the site uses Georgia / Helvetica / Arial. It still works.
